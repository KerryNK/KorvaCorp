Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Korva Global Corporation (“Korva,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information you provide when you visit our website or contact us.

By using this website or submitting information through it, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you use our website or contact us:

  • Your name.
  • Email address.
  • Organization or company name.
  • The nature of your inquiry.
  • Any information you choose to include in your message.
  • Information you voluntarily provide through our contact form, email, or other communication channels.

We collect only the information you choose to provide when contacting us.

2. How we use your information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or request.
  • Assess potential partnerships, collaborations, or business opportunities.
  • Communicate with you about Korva, its subsidiaries, products, events, or services.
  • Improve the performance, security, and usability of our website.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations where applicable.
  • Protect Korva, its team, users, systems, and intellectual property.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable, we process personal information based on one or more of the following:

  • Your consent, where you have provided information voluntarily.
  • Our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries and operating our business.
  • Compliance with legal or regulatory obligations.
  • The performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps, where relevant.

4. Sharing your information

We may share your information only where necessary with:

  • Trusted service providers who help us operate our website, email, analytics, security, or communication systems.
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers, accountants, or auditors, where necessary.
  • Relevant authorities, where required by law or to protect legal rights.
  • A Korva subsidiary, business unit, or authorized team member where necessary to respond to your inquiry.

We require third parties who process personal information on our behalf to handle it appropriately and securely.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain business records, meet legal requirements, and protect our rights and interests.

6. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent for your data. To make a request, contact us at legal@korvacorp.com.

8. Cookies and analytics

Korva Global Corporation does not currently use cookies, analytics tools, or third-party tracking scripts on this website. We collect only the information you choose to provide when contacting us.

9. Children’s privacy

This website is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
